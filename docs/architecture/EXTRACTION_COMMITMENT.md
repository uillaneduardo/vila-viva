# Extraction commitment (#44/#50)

An optional serializable intent records source/mine, item, start and last progress ticks. Existing urgent deliveries and survival precede continuation; no new authority, reservations or items are created. Surface output remains a physical drop, collected locally before the next extraction. Discretionary transport is deferred while capacity and a reachable destination permit a batch; energy margin uses route steps plus rest threshold. Underground mining continues into the existing local stock until full/exhausted or interrupted. Accepted moves and actions renew progress; rejection, 24 ticks without progress, invalid targets or survival clear intent. Old snapshots omit the field. Limits are provisional; concurrent storage reservations, generalized cooldowns, specialized transport and balancing remain #50/#65. CI and multiseed required before integration.
